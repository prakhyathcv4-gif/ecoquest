Sustainable Engineering

Rainwater Harvesting: Closed-Loop Hydrology

Capturing rain runoff before it hits asphalt and stormwater channels provides abundant, chemical-free soft water for landscaping and hygiene.

1
🌧️

Rain Catchment

Clean rooftop gutters intercept pristine precipitation during storm events.

2
⚙️

First-Flush Diverter

Initial dirty runoff with dust and roof debris is flushed away before entering the cistern.

3
🛢️

Storage Cistern

Enclosed, light-sealed storage tanks prevent algae formation and mosquito breeding.

4
🌱

Targeted Usage

Submersible pumps distribute chlorine-free water to gardens, campus lawns, and toilet tanks.

🌍

Why Freshwater Matters

97.5% of all water on Earth is salty ocean water. Of the remaining 2.5% freshwater, nearly 70% is frozen in polar caps. The tiny fraction remaining sustains all 8 billion humans, farms, and terrestrial biodiversity.

🚯

Preventing Water Pollution

Never pour paints, motor oil, cooking oil, or harsh synthetic chemical cleaners down the sink drain. Urban stormwater runoff carries untreated toxins straight into local rivers, destroying river ecosystems.

🚰

Low-Flow Fixtures

Installing screw-on sink faucet aerators (costing $3) mixes air into the stream, delivering high rinse pressure while cutting physical water volume by 40% immediately.
