Modal Hierarchy

Six Sustainable Transit Options

Choosing the right tool for each journey maximizes efficiency, saves money, and improves personal well-being.

🚶

Walking

The purest zero-emission commute. Ideal for trips under 2 km. It improves cardiovascular health, burns stress hormones, and costs zero dollars.

🚲

Cycling & e-Bikes

Bicycles are the most energetically efficient machines ever invented. Cover 5-10 km across university campuses with zero fossil combustion and zero parking fees.

🚌

Public Transit

Buses and shuttles maximize road space by transporting dozens of commuters in a single vehicle corridor, drastically reducing urban gridlock.

🚆

Electric Rail & Metro

Heavy rail runs along dedicated steel tracks with minimal rolling friction, drawing clean electricity from centralized regional power grids.

🚗

Carpooling & Rideshare

When transit is unavailable, filling four passenger seats in one vehicle divides total per-person emissions and fuel expenses by 75%.

⚡

Electric Vehicles

EVs produce zero direct tailpipe smog, nitrogen oxides, or soot. As the electrical grid greens with solar and wind, EVs become cleaner each month.
