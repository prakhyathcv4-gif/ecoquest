Plastic-Free Day

Avoid single-use plastic for one full day. Decline plastic bags, disposable cutlery, straws, and cling wrap.

🚲 +30 pts

Green Commute

Walk, cycle, or use public transport (bus/metro) for all your campus or work journeys instead of driving alone.

⚡ +15 pts

Save Energy

Switch off unnecessary lights, unplug standby phone/laptop chargers, and maximize natural daylight study hours.

💧 +20 pts

Save Water

Reduce unnecessary water usage. Take a swift 5-minute shower and turn off the tap completely while brushing your teeth.

🥗 +25 pts

Meatless Green Meals

Eat nutritious 100% plant-based meals today (lentils, beans, tofu, seasonal veggies) to drastically slash your food carbon footprint.

🍱 +20 pts

Zero Food Waste Day

Plan portions accurately, eat all leftovers for lunch, and avoid tossing edible food into the trash can.

🧺 +15 pts

Cold Wash Laundry

Do your laundry load using cold water instead of hot. 90% of washing machine energy goes entirely into heating water!

✂️ +30 pts

Upcycle or Repair

Mend a torn garment, repurpose an empty glass jar into a container, or build a plant pot from an old PET bottle.
