Sustainable Food

One-third of all food produced globally is wasted. Discover how plant-forward diets, local harvests, and portion planning heal both personal health and planetary ecosystems.

Smart Swaps

Better Choice Comparison

See how straightforward everyday grocery swaps slash plastic packaging, transport emissions, and wasted food.

Conventional Choice

Plastic-Overpackaged Produce

Apples and bell peppers sealed on styrofoam trays in PVC plastic shrink-wrap. Non-recyclable film that chokes municipal landfills.

✓ Better Choice

Loose Produce in Reusable Mesh Bags

Pick unpackaged loose fruit directly into washable mesh cotton produce sacks. Zero single-use film generated, and cheaper per pound!

Conventional Choice

Air-Freighted Out-of-Season Berries

Strawberries flown 6,000 miles across oceans in winter months, requiring continuous refrigeration and massive aviation fuel burn.

✓ Better Choice

Local Seasonal Farm Market Harvest

Produce grown by regional farmers harvested at peak ripeness. Minimizes "food miles," retains superior micronutrients, and funds local agrarian economies.

Conventional Choice

Unplanned Bulk Cooking & Spoiled Leftovers

Cooking excess portions without storage plans, resulting in half-eaten meals moldering in dorm fridges and ending up in the garbage.

✓ Better Choice

Proper Portion Planning & Freezer Mason Jars

Cook exact meals or freeze extra stew in glass containers immediately. Saves money, eliminates waste, and provides instant ready-to-eat college dinners.

The Power of Plant-Based Diets

Agricultural livestock requires enormous calorie inputs (grain and soy) to produce meat calories. Shifting toward plant-forward proteins—such as lentils, chickpeas, black beans, edamame, and seeds—demands up to 90% less land and 85% less freshwater.
